# rwedev 0.0.0.9000

* Follows RWE standards `v2` (`Config/rwe/standards: v2`) and has the managed
  `air.toml`.
* No templates are bundled in the package: the templates in rwe-ecosystem's
  `templates/` folder are the only source.
* `rwe_standards_version()` returns `"v2"`, the version of the RWE standards
  this release implements.
